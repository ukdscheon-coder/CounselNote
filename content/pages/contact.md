We are a small UK team and we read every message. We aim to reply within two working days.

## Buying, quotations and invoices

For licence purchases, school or trust quotations, purchase orders and VAT invoices, email **[sales@counselnote.uk](mailto:sales@counselnote.uk)**. Please include your school or organisation name, the licence tier you are interested in, and your invoice address if you would like to pay by invoice.

## Technical support

For help installing or using CounselNote, email **[support@counselnote.uk](mailto:support@counselnote.uk)**. Please tell us your operating system (Windows or macOS) and CounselNote version, which you can find in Settings.

**Please never send pupil information, session notes or your vault passphrase by email.** We cannot access or recover your data, and we will never ask for your passphrase.

## Privacy and data protection

For questions about this website's privacy notice or how we handle purchase information, email **[privacy@counselnote.uk](mailto:privacy@counselnote.uk)**.
